OutLier Treatment:

Instead of writing:

"There are no erroneous values."

Use:

"No obvious erroneous/suspicious values were identified based on the checks performed."

Why?

Because we're doing observational data analysis, not auditing the database's source system.

We can establish:

internally consistent
plausible
suspicious
impossible
sentinel
likely erroneous

But rarely can we prove that a real-world value is 100% correct.
